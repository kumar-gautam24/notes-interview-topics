# 30-Day AI + Backend Preparation Pack

Four documents that work as one system: the roadmap says *when*, the cheat sheet says *what to
know*, the question bank says *how you get tested*, and the tracker is *proof you actually
learned it*. They share one L1–L5 depth rubric.

## Read in this order

1. **[01-30-day-roadmap.md](01-30-day-roadmap.md)** — the day-by-day sequence, four weeks.
2. **[03-cheat-sheet.md](03-cheat-sheet.md)** — 29 mental models, for daily review.
3. **[02-question-bank.md](02-question-bank.md)** — 612 questions, graded L1→L5, in the roadmap's topic order.
4. **[04-evidence-tracker.md](04-evidence-tracker.md)** — checklists, five lab specs, and a readiness gate.

## Scope

Centred on **Python/FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Redis and queues · distributed reliability · LLM tool
calling and agents · RAG/pgvector/evaluation · model serving · Docker/AWS · system design.**

The guiding rule throughout: don't claim a technology you can't defend independently under three
rounds of "why?" — including anything an AI coding tool wrote for you.
